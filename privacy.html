Privacy

Privacy notice.

How SLIID handles information submitted through this website.

The Sri Lanka Institute of Interior Designers (SLIID) respects your privacy and uses personal information only for the purpose for which it is submitted.

Information we collect

Contact enquiries may include your name, email address, telephone number, enquiry type and message. Membership applications may also include professional or educational information and documents you choose to upload.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, assess membership applications, administer Institute activities and maintain appropriate records. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and access

Submissions are stored in the Institute’s secure administration system once that service is connected. Access is limited to authorised administrators and relevant members of the Executive Council.

Your choices

Do not submit sensitive information unless it is required for your application. You may ask SLIID to correct or delete information, subject to legal and administrative requirements.

Contact

Privacy requests may be sent through the Contact page when the secure enquiry service is available, or delivered to 601, 6th Floor, Janajaya City, Nawala Road, Rajagiriya.

This interim notice should be reviewed and approved by the Executive Council before the public forms are enabled.
