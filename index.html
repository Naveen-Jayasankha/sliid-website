The Institute

Advancing interior design in Sri Lanka.

The Sri Lanka Institute of Interior Designers (SLIID) is the national professional body for interior designers, incorporated by Act of Parliament No. 2 of 1998.

We promote professional excellence, ethical practice and the value of good interior design in creating better places to live, work and gather.
