01 Our institute

A home for ideas that transform how people live.

The Sri Lanka Institute of Interior Designers brings the profession together—creating space for learning, dialogue and responsible design practice.

We believe the strongest interiors are grounded in culture, shaped by human needs and built for a more thoughtful future.

Read about the Institute →
Established by Parliament

Sri Lanka Institute of Interior Designers was incorporated in Sri Lanka by an Act of Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on 27 February 1998, under Act No. 2 of 1998.
